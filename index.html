THE HERITAGE

The Authentic Taste That Conquered Gujarat

Jay Mahakali Sev Usal has captured the hearts of food connoisseurs across the state with its distinctive red Tari recipe. Unlike typical street food stalls, Mahakali perfected a slow-simmered, spice-layered broth that strikes the exact balance between rich aromatics, fiery heat, and comforting savory satisfaction.

Today, that identical authentic taste is proudly served at Shop No. 9, Supan Serenity, Science City Road, Sola, Ahmedabad. Freshly made every morning with pure besan sev, tender dried green peas, and genuine Amul dairy.

🌿 100% Pure Besan Sev

Fried to golden perfection with zero preservatives or artificial coloring.

🔥 The Secret Masala

Ground from whole cloves, coriander, cumin, and dry Kashmiri chilies.

🧅 Fresh Spring Greens

Finely chopped spring onions that cut through the richness of the Tari.

🍞 Bakery Bun Tradition

Soft, fresh buns that soak every nuance of the flavorful raso.
